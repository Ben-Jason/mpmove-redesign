Putting it live

The new page goes on top of your current page inside systeme.io. It uses the sign-up form you already have, so your sign-ups, your emails and your thank-you page keep working the way they do now. Your link in bio stays the same.

You need: a computer (not your phone) and your systeme.io login.

  1. Copy the code

    Press the button. The whole new page is copied, ready to paste.

    If nothing was copied, open the file paste-into-systeme.txt that came with this, select everything in it and copy that.

  2. Open your page in systeme.io

    Log in, go to Funnels, open the funnel that holds your mpmove.com page, click the page with the sign-up form, then click Edit page.

  3. Add one block and paste

    • In the panel on the left, find the element called Raw HTML.
    • Drag it onto the page, right at the top, above your headline.
    • Click the new block, click Edit code, paste, and click Save.
    • Save the page with the save button at the top of the editor.

    Leave everything else on the page exactly as it is. Do not delete your old headline, pictures or form. The new block covers them, and it needs your form to be there.

  4. Test it before you tell anyone

    Open mpmove.com in a private (incognito) window. The preview inside the editor does not show the new block. Only the real page does.

    Sign up with your own name, email and phone number. Then check three things:

    • You land on your usual thank-you page.
    • Your usual emails arrive.
    • You appear under Contacts in systeme.io.

    Please do not skip this. The new page has been checked on its own, but it has not been tried inside your systeme.io account yet. This test is the proof that sign-ups still reach you.

If you want your old page back

Open the editor again, click the Raw HTML block, delete it, and save. Your old page is back exactly as it was, because nothing else was changed.

Worth knowing

  • Phone number. The new form asks for a phone number because your current form requires one. If you would rather not ask for it, delete the phone field from your old form in the editor. The new page then stops asking for it by itself.
  • Wording to check. The consent line still names Lizelle de Kock and Digital Magnetic System, and one section still says "Trusted by thousands". Both were copied from your current page. The line at the very bottom about results not being guaranteed is new.
  • Changes. If you want different words, colours or sections, tell Ben-Jason. You will get a new code to paste over the old one, in the same block.
  • If the test sign-up shows an error about the phone number, delete the phone field from your old form as described above, save, and test again.
  • If the test fails another way or something looks wrong, delete the block to get your old page back and send Ben-Jason a screenshot.
